# Lello

## Product vision

Lello is a personal library for organizing a reader's life: books they want to read,
are reading, and have finished.

The product helps people keep a clear history of their reading, revisit their favorite
books, and decide what to read next. Notes, reflections, and audio recordings are
optional layers attached to a book, not the center of the experience.

## Problem

Readers often lose track of what they have read, what they want to read next, and how
they felt about each book. Existing reading lists can become passive catalogs without
helping people build a useful personal history.

Lello makes that history simple to maintain and easy to revisit.

## Target user

Initial target users:

- people who read non-fiction
- people studying through books
- people who want to retain ideas better
- people who want a personal reading memory system

## MVP scope

The MVP should focus on:

- personal library
- wishlist, currently reading, and completed books
- book details and external book search
- reading start and completion dates
- simple rating from one to five stars
- optional personal comment or review
- filters for status, completion year, and rating
- a clear reading history

Notes and audio remain available inside a book as optional features. They should not
interrupt the core reading workflow or dominate the main library experience.

## Out of scope for MVP

Do not implement yet:

- social network
- public profiles
- rankings
- book recommendation algorithm
- marketplace or affiliate links
- complex AI assistant
- mobile app
- advanced analytics
- complex permissions

## Core principle

The value is in maintaining a useful personal library, not in having a perfect external
book database or requiring users to document every reading moment.

The primary loop is:

1. Add a book to the wishlist.
2. Move it to currently reading.
3. Mark it as completed.
4. Add an optional rating and comment.
5. Revisit the library to choose what to read next or remember past reading.

## UX direction

The UI should feel:

- minimal
- calm
- focused
- modern
- similar in quality to Notion, Readwise, Linear, OpenAI, and Supabase

## Language

User-facing UI should be in Portuguese.
Code, comments, docs, variables, functions, and file names should be in English.
